# Social Media Checklist for College Students



### Do's

1.Think before posting: ensure your content is respectful and appropriate.

2.Use privacy settings to control who can see your posts.

3.Share positive and constructive content that adds value.

4.Verify information before sharing to avoid spreading misinformation.

5.Balance online time with offline academic and personal responsibilities.



### Don'ts

1.Don't post offensive, discriminatory, or harmful content.

2.Don't overshare personal information that could compromise your safety.

3.Don't engage in cyberbullying or negative online behavior.

4.Don't rely solely on social media for news or academic resources.

5.Don't let social media distract you from studies and imortant commitments.
